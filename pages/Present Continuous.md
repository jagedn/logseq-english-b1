- ## When to Use It
  - Actions happening right now at the moment of speaking.
  - Temporary situations or states.
  - Changing or developing situations.
  - Fixed future arrangements (with a specified time context).

- ## Grammatical Structure
  - ### Affirmative
    - **Structure:** `Subject + am / is / are + Verb (-ing)`
    - *Examples:* I am working, She is reading, They are playing.
  - ### Negative
    - **Structure:** `Subject + am / is / are + not + Verb (-ing)`
    - *Note:* `am not` (I'm not), `is not` -> `isn't`, `are not` -> `aren't`.
  - ### Interrogative
    - **Structure:** `Am / Is / Are + Subject + Verb (-ing) + ?`

- ## Examples
  - Look! It **is snowing** outside.
  - I **am staying** at a hotel this week.
  - He **isn't studying** right now; he's sleeping.
  - **Are you meeting** Sarah tomorrow afternoon?

- ## Spelling Rules for Verb + -ing
  - **General rule:** Base verb + *-ing* (e.g., *work* -> *working*).
  - **Verbs ending in silent -e:** Drop *-e* and add *-ing* (e.g., *write* -> *writing*).
  - **Short verbs (CVC - Consonant + Vowel + Consonant):** Double the final consonant (e.g., *run* -> *running*, *stop* -> *stopping*).
  - **Verbs ending in -ie:** Change *-ie* to *-y* + *-ing* (e.g., *die* -> *dying*, *lie* -> *lying*).
